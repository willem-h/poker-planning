Estimate together, without a server

Your browser opens an iroh endpoint and joins a gossip topic. Estimates travel directly between participants, end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is stored anywhere, and the room disappears when the last tab closes.

How it works

No host
Every participant holds the whole room and merges what the others broadcast, so nobody's departure ends the session.
Votes stay hidden
While a round is open, peers publish only a hash of their estimate. The values are released on reveal and checked against those hashes.
Relayed, not readable
Browsers can't send UDP, so traffic hops through an iroh relay. The relay moves encrypted QUIC packets and cannot read them.
